Our Role

Independent advice. Not construction execution.

Fair Point Consulting is an independent consulting practice. We provide advisory, coordination, and review-support services; we do not act as a general contractor, provide construction labour, or undertake construction work.

Our role is to help clients and project teams think through the details, understand their options, and make better-informed decisions.

What we do

Experienced support at every stage.

Flexible consulting services designed around project requirements — from early planning and technical review through construction and closeout. Fairview provides advice and support; construction execution remains with the appointed project team and contractors.

Shop Drawing Review Support

Construction Consulting

Meet the owner

Wendel Baker

With 20 years of experience in the construction industry, Wendel Baker brings a practical, hands-on understanding of the work, the people and the decisions that shape successful projects.

Wendel earned his Red Seal certification at an exceptionally young age, reflecting an early commitment to his trade and to developing his expertise. His experience provides Fairview with a strong foundation in construction knowledge and a practical approach to solving project challenges.

His philosophy is straightforward: understand the work, communicate clearly, identify challenges early and help clients find solutions that make sense.
